# Day 34 - Marketing Detective Pro

## Overview

Marketing Detective Pro is a detective-themed marketing analysis simulator where players investigate real-world-inspired marketing challenges, analyze campaign evidence, identify root causes, and solve business cases through structured decision-making.

The application transforms marketing education into an engaging investigative experience while reinforcing key concepts such as customer acquisition, conversion optimization, retention, branding, engagement, and campaign performance analysis.

## Features

- 15+ unique marketing investigation cases
- Interactive drag-and-drop evidence board
- Detective notebook for collecting insights
- Customer feedback and competitor intelligence panels
- Case-solving and verdict system
- XP, ranks, and achievement progression
- Marketing KPI learning center
- Theme selection including Claude Orange
- Local progress tracking with LocalStorage
- Mobile-responsive design
- Fully offline functionality
- Single-file HTML implementation

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- LocalStorage
