# Day 30 - Supply Chain Builder

## Overview

Supply Chain Builder is an interactive business simulation designed to teach supply chain fundamentals in a beginner-friendly way. Players build a complete supply chain for a randomly generated company by making strategic decisions about suppliers, factory locations, warehouses, transportation, and inventory management.

The simulator explains every concept before decisions are made and demonstrates how each choice affects real business outcomes.

## Features

● Random company generation for every playthrough

● Beginner-friendly supply chain education system

● Supplier strategy decision-making

● Factory location planning

● Warehouse network optimization

● Transportation method selection

● Inventory management strategies

● Real-time business metric tracking

● Live KPI dashboard with animated progress bars

● Trade-off explanations after every decision

● Final executive supply chain report

● Strengths, weaknesses, and risk analysis

● Personalized improvement recommendations

● Replay system with new company scenarios

## Challenge

This project focused on transforming complex supply chain concepts into a beginner-friendly interactive learning experience. The challenge was balancing education and simulation while ensuring every decision had measurable business consequences and clear real-world explanations.
