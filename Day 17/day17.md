# Day 17 - Vehicle Cost & Fuel Analysis Dashboard

## What I Learned:-

Today I learned how AI can analyze raw vehicle datasets and transform them into professional dashboards containing fuel cost analysis, maintenance insights, environmental metrics, and data-driven recommendations.

This task helped me understand how business intelligence dashboards convert structured data into meaningful visual insights that support decision-making and performance evaluation.

## My Work:-

• Built a complete Vehicle Cost & Fuel Analysis Dashboard.

• Analyzed vehicle fuel and maintenance datasets.

• Calculated Fuel Cost per KM for different fuel types.

• Compared CO₂ emissions across multiple fuel categories.

• Evaluated maintenance cost efficiency.

• Created Fuel Efficiency and Mileage comparisons.

• Generated Vehicle Age Group Cost Analysis.

• Built multiple SVG-based charts and visualizations.

• Created Fuel Ranking and Performance Analysis.

• Generated insights and recommendations based on dataset calculations.

• Designed a modern glassmorphism dashboard interface.

• Implemented responsive design for desktop and mobile devices.

## Key Learnings:-

• Data visualization improves understanding of complex datasets.

• Business dashboards help convert raw data into actionable insights.

• Fuel efficiency analysis supports cost optimization decisions.

• Environmental metrics play an important role in vehicle evaluation.

• AI can automate data analysis and dashboard generation workflows.

• Interactive dashboards improve presentation and reporting quality.

## Conclusion:-

This task helped me understand how AI can be used for data analysis, visualization, and business intelligence reporting. 
I learned how structured datasets can be transformed into professional dashboards that provide meaningful insights, comparisons, and recommendations for decision-making.
