❓ 5 Smart Interview Questions to Ask

1. Role Clarity & Responsibilities

"I see this role involves research, development, deployment, and generative AI work. Can you break down: (a) What are my top 3 priorities in the first 3 months? (b) What % of time do you expect on each major responsibility? (c) Are there any other roles doing similar work, or am I the only one?"

💡 Why: Reveals if role is realistic or if you'll be spread too thin.

2. Skill Requirements Authenticity

"I see 12 required skills listed. For a fresh graduate joining, which 3-4 are absolutely critical on day 1? Which can I learn on the job? What's your on-boarding training like?"

💡 Why: Tests if they've realistically assessed candidate needs or if job description is inflated.

3. Hybrid Work & Work-Life Balance

"You mention hybrid work. Specifically: (a) How many days/week in office? (b) Can I vary this or is it fixed? (c) What are typical working hours? (d) Do you expect on-call or weekend work? (e) What's your vacation policy?"

💡 Why: "Hybrid" can mean anything. Get specifics before signing.

4. Compensation & Equity

"What's the salary range for this role? Are there signing bonuses, stock options, or performance bonuses? What's your benefits package (health, 401k match, learning budget)?"

💡 Why: Never accept a job without knowing compensation. Research market rates first (₹6-15L for junior AI roles in Bangalore).

5. Company Stability & Growth

"I'm excited about the role. A few questions about company health: (a) What's your current funding status? (b) What's your runway? (c) What's been your employee retention rate in the last 1-2 years? (d) Are you hiring or planning layoffs?"

💡 Why: Young startups fail. You need to know the company is stable enough to pay you.
