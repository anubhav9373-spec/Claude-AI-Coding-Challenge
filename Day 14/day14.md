# Day 14 - AI Job Red Flag Detector

## What I Learned:-

Today I learned how AI can be used to analyze job descriptions, identify hiring risks, detect workplace red flags, and support smarter career decisions.

This task helped me understand how AI-powered career intelligence systems can evaluate company information, hiring practices, work culture signals, and job requirements before applying.

## My Work:-

- Built an AI Job Red Flag Detector dashboard.
- Analyzed job descriptions and company information.
- Generated an overall risk assessment score.
- Identified hiring and workplace red flags.
- Evaluated company stability and growth risks.
- Analyzed remote and hybrid work authenticity.
- Generated smart interview validation questions.
- Created a professional dark-themed HTML dashboard.
- Produced actionable job application recommendations.
- Delivered a structured risk analysis report.

## Key Learnings:-

- AI can help job seekers make better career decisions.
- Hidden risks often exist inside job descriptions.
- Company analysis is as important as role analysis.
- Interview questions can validate hiring transparency.
- AI-powered career tools can reduce job search uncertainty.

## Conclusion:-

This task helped me understand how AI can act as a career intelligence assistant by evaluating job opportunities beyond surface-level descriptions.

I learned how AI-powered analysis can identify risks, improve decision-making, and help candidates approach job opportunities more strategically.
