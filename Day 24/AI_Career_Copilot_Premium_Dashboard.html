Growth Risks & Mitigation

🔴 CAC Inflation Risk
Probability: 65% | YouTube costs rising 20-30% YoY. Blended CAC could reach ₹500+ by Q3 2024. Mitigation: Focus on organic content (₹100 CAC) and college partnerships (₹200 CAC). Target college mix to 50% of new users.
🟡 College MOU Friction
Probability: 55% | Procurement cycles 6-12 months. Realistic target: 3-5 colleges, not 20. Mitigation: Personal relationships with placement directors. Free pilot → paid license model. Start with Tier-2/3 colleges (faster decisions).
🔴 Conversion Collapse
Probability: 40% | If conversion <10%, Year 1 revenue halves to ₹25-30L. Mitigation: Outcome guarantees ("job or money back"). Mentorship tier at ₹999/mo. Build job placement tracking early.
🟡 Viral Loop Failure
Probability: 50% | Viral coefficient assumption (>1.2) untested. Reality: typical edtech 0.8-1.0. Mitigation: In-app referral incentives. Success story amplification. Weekly viral coefficient tracking.
