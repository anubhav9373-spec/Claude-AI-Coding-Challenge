# Day 24 – Business Strategy & Sustainability Analysis

## Project
AI Career Copilot – Startup Intelligence Platform

## Objective
Analyze whether the startup can become a sustainable business by evaluating revenue model, GTM strategy, customer acquisition, competitive moat, investment readiness, and long-term viability.

## Deliverables
- Business Reality Check
- Business Model Canvas
- Revenue & Pricing Strategy
- Go-To-Market Strategy
- Customer Acquisition Plan
- First 100 Users Roadmap
- Competitive Moat Analysis
- Reverse SWOT Analysis
- Investor Scorecard
- Founder Action Sheet
- Interactive Premium Dashboard

## Key Learnings
- Revenue matters more than vanity metrics.
- Distribution is more important than product features.
- Customer acquisition costs determine scalability.
- College partnerships can become a strong acquisition channel.
- Sustainable growth requires validated unit economics.
- A startup without a moat is vulnerable to larger competitors.
- PMF must be validated before aggressive scaling.
- Data and distribution can become long-term competitive advantages.

## Outcome
Built a premium interactive dashboard presenting startup sustainability analysis, investment readiness assessment, GTM strategy, revenue model, growth risks, and founder action plans.
