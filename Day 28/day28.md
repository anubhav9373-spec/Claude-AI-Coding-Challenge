# Day 28 - Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

## Overview

Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator is an interactive healthcare operations training application that helps users understand the complete hospital admission process through a realistic workflow experience.

Users take on the role of a Hospital Admission Coordinator and manage critical admission activities including Prior Authorization review, insurance verification, bed assignment, physician orders, clinical documentation, patient consent, nursing coordination, and utilization review.

The simulator evaluates admission readiness and provides realistic healthcare operational outcomes based on user decisions.

## Features

- Interactive hospital admission workflow
