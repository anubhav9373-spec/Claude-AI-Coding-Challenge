AB Talks • 60-Day Claude AI Challenge

From AI Curiosity
to Shipping a Product.

A 60-day progression through AI foundations, prompting, research, automation, product thinking, multimodal workflows, knowledge systems and agentic concepts — culminating in a complete software-development capstone.

PHASE 01

Foundations → Effective Prompting

Built a practical understanding of AI capabilities and learned to turn vague intentions into constrained, testable instructions.

PHASE 02

Reasoning → Research Workflows

Moved beyond one-shot answers into structured investigation, evaluation, iteration and evidence-driven workflows.

PHASE 03

Automation → Product Thinking

Started treating AI as part of a workflow and learned to define users, problems, scope, requirements and useful outcomes before building.

PHASE 04

Multimodal → Knowledge Systems

Worked across richer inputs and increasingly sophisticated projects, connecting AI generation with information organization and usable interfaces.

PHASE 05

Agents → Collaboration

Explored autonomous-agent architectures and multi-agent collaboration, shifting from isolated prompts toward coordinated AI-assisted systems.

PHASE 06

Build → Debug → Verify

The key shift: generated output stopped being the finish line. Code and product decisions had to be inspected, tested, corrected and proven in the running application.

FINAL PRACTICAL MILESTONE

LearnInsight AI • v1.0.0

The 10-day capstone brought the accumulated skills into one SDLC: requirements, architecture, implementation, Gemini integration, document parsing, persistence, frontend integration, debugging, edge-case testing, deployment and release preparation.
