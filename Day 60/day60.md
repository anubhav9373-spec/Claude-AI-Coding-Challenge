---

## 🧠 LearnInsight AI

LearnInsight AI allows users to upload **PDF, DOCX, and TXT documents** and uses **Gemini AI** to transform their content into useful learning resources.

### ✨ Core Features

- 📄 AI-generated document summaries
- 💡 Simplified explanations
- 🧠 Quiz generation
- 🗂️ Flashcards
- 📝 AI-generated study notes
- 📚 Document history
- 📁 PDF, DOCX & TXT document processing
- 🌐 Production deployment
