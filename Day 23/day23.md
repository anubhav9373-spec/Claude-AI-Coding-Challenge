# Day 23 - Customer & MVP Blueprint

## What I Learned:-

Today I learned how successful startups identify ideal customers before building products.

I explored customer research frameworks, buyer personas, customer journeys, MVP planning, feature prioritization, pricing hypotheses, and product validation strategies.

This task demonstrated how product managers and startup founders can reduce risk by understanding customer needs before investing heavily in development.

## My Work:-

● Built a complete Customer & MVP Blueprint Dashboard.

● Defined the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

● Created a detailed Buyer Persona.

● Identified Top 10 Customer Pain Points.

● Mapped the Customer Journey from Awareness to Retention.

● Analyzed Key Customer Objections.

● Identified Key Buying Triggers.

● Generated MVP Recommendations.

● Defined what should be built first.

● Identified features that should not be built initially.

● Created MVP Success Metrics.

● Built a MoSCoW Prioritization Matrix.

● Developed Pricing Hypotheses.

● Evaluated Top 5 Business Risks.

● Created a 30-Day MVP Execution Plan.

● Generated a Founder Action Sheet.

● Calculated Customer Clarity, PMF Potential, Problem Severity, and MVP Readiness Scores.

● Designed a premium customer intelligence dashboard.

## Key Learnings:-

● Customer understanding should come before product development.

● Pain points drive product adoption.

● Buyer personas help improve product-market fit.

● MVPs should focus on validating assumptions quickly.

● MoSCoW prioritization helps avoid feature overload.

● Pricing should be tested through hypotheses.

● Customer journeys reveal friction points and growth opportunities.

● AI can accelerate customer research and product planning workflows.

## Conclusion:-

This project helped me understand how customer discovery, MVP planning, and product strategy work together to reduce startup risk. 
I learned how AI can be used to transform customer insights into actionable product decisions and execution plans.
