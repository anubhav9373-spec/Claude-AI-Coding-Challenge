What this tool does

This is your Personal AI Playbook — a system for building reusable AI prompts instead of retyping instructions every time. It's built around how you work as a software engineer: writing code, debugging, designing architecture, writing docs, and building AI agents.

  • Dashboard — browse and launch ready-made workflows, grouped by the kind of engineering task they help with.
  • Prompt Builder — assemble a one-off prompt from labeled building blocks (role, context, constraints, etc.) with a live preview.
  • Loop Builder — turn a prompt into a self-checking loop that verifies its own output against criteria before it stops.
  • My Workflows — everything you save, search, favorite, duplicate, or edit, stored only on this device.

About this playbook

Built for: software engineering · expert AI usage · multiple models · biggest bottleneck: getting AI to follow multi-step processes reliably. Everything here is designed to reduce re-explaining context and to make self-checking a built-in habit rather than an afterthought.

What is this?

The idea

Most people rewrite similar AI prompts from scratch every time, and lose the ones that worked. This playbook turns your best prompting patterns into reusable systems: small labeled blocks (role, context, constraints…) you combine instead of hundreds of one-off prompts.

Dashboard

Ready-made workflows grouped by task — code writing, debugging, architecture, docs, code review, and agent design. Click any card to open it, favorite the ones you use often, and duplicate to make your own variant.

Prompt Builder

Assembles a single prompt from building blocks. Each block has a plain-language explanation of what it controls, right where you pick it. A live preview on the right shows exactly what will be sent to the AI.

Loop Builder

For tasks where you want the AI to check and improve its own answer before handing it back — e.g. reviewing its own code against a checklist. You define the goal, how success is judged, how it should improve, and when it must stop.

My Workflows

Your saved library. Search, filter by category, favorite, duplicate, edit, or delete. Stored with your browser's local storage — nothing leaves your device.
