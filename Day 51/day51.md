# Day 51 – LearnInsight AI | Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## Overview

Day 51 marks the beginning of the 10-day capstone of the AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge.

Instead of writing code, this day focused on product discovery, defining a realistic MVP, and creating a complete implementation roadmap for the remaining nine development days.

The selected capstone project is **LearnInsight AI**, an AI-powered Learning Workspace that transforms study documents into structured learning resources using Google Gemini AI.

---

## Project Summary

LearnInsight AI helps users upload PDF, DOCX, and TXT documents and automatically generates:

- AI Summary
- Simplified Explanation
- Quiz
- Flashcards
- AI Notes

All generated learning resources are organized inside a clean dashboard with document history.
