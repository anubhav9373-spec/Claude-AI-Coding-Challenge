# Day 29 - Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## Overview

Operation Lifeline is an interactive supply chain crisis simulation that places users in the role of a COO responsible for navigating enterprise-wide disruptions.

Every playthrough generates a different company profile, crisis scenario, negotiation outcome, and business result, creating a realistic decision-making experience.

## Features

● Random company generation with unique operational profiles

● Multiple supply chain crisis scenarios including supplier bankruptcy, factory disruptions, cyberattacks, port strikes, and raw material shortages

● Crisis War Room where users select strategic response actions

● Multi-round supplier negotiation simulator

● CEO boardroom decision-making challenges

● AI-powered operations investment strategies

● Real-time business metrics tracking

● Enterprise-style executive dashboard

● Multiple possible business outcomes based on user decisions

● Fully replayable simulation experience

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript (Vanilla JS)
- Responsive Design
- Dynamic State Management

## Learning Outcomes

- Supply Chain Risk Management
- Crisis Response Planning
- Supplier Relationship Management

## Challenge

This project focused on building a realistic enterprise simulation where every decision has consequences. 

The challenge was designing interconnected systems that combine crisis management, supplier negotiations, leadership decisions, and AI investments into a single interactive experience.
