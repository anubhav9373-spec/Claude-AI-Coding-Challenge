# Day 21 - Digital Privacy Intelligence Dashboard

## What I Learned:-

Today I learned how AI can be used to analyze digital ecosystems, estimate privacy exposure, identify tracking risks, and generate intelligence dashboards that help visualize a user's digital footprint.

This task helped me understand how modern privacy analytics platforms combine data visualization, risk assessment, behavioral analysis, and actionable recommendations to improve digital security awareness.

• Digital Twin modeling can estimate likely behavioral patterns.
