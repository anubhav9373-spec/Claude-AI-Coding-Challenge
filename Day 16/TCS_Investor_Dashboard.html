Watch Points & Risks

US Litigation Exposure
US Supreme Court rejected appeal in DXC case. Total exposure now ₹220M (₹150M provisioned + ₹70M new provision in Q1 FY27). Manageable but requires monitoring.
North America Softness
Recent quarters show regional disparities with North America (largest market) facing demand slowness. Client budget constraints persist post-macro pressures.
Moderate Growth Pace
10.2% revenue CAGR (5Y) modest vs sector. Scale constraints may limit acceleration. AI ramp-up critical for future growth acceleration.
Attrition & Wage Inflation
Rising SG&A expenses and wage pressures noted in Q3. Employee retention in tight talent market remains ongoing challenge.
Valuation Multiple Risk
Despite attractiveness at 14.9x P/E, multiple expansion potential may be limited given scale. Earnings growth critical for value creation.
