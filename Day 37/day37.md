# Day 37 - Task Compass

## Overview

Task Compass is an interactive organizational learning simulator designed to teach how work flows through real organizations.

Instead of testing job titles or memorization, the application helps users understand ownership, delegation, collaboration, escalation, and workflow management through realistic workplace scenarios.

Users can explore different workplace environments and learn how teams coordinate to solve problems, route tasks, and collaborate effectively.

Built as part of the 60 Days Claude AI Challenge.
