# Day 31 - AI Supply Chain Control Tower

## Overview

AI Supply Chain Control Tower is an interactive operations simulation that places users in the role of a Supply Chain Director. 
Players make strategic decisions across risk management, inventory optimization, sustainability, supplier diversification, warehouse networks, and technology adoption while monitoring real-time business KPIs.

## Features

- Interactive supply chain decision-making simulation
- AI Advisor with strategic recommendations
- Real-time KPI tracking
- Before vs After performance comparison
- Multiple business optimization scenarios
- Dynamic scoring and grading system
- Responsive enterprise dashboard
- Modern dark-themed UI
- Replayable simulation experience

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript

## Learning Outcomes

- Supply Chain Risk Management
- Inventory Optimization Strategies
- Supplier Diversification Planning
- Warehouse Network Design
- Sustainability in Supply Chains
- Supply Chain Technology Adoption
- KPI-Based Decision Making

## Challenge

Built a complete supply chain optimization simulator that demonstrates how strategic operational decisions impact profitability, risk, resilience, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction.
