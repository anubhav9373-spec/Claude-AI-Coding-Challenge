Headline — 3 options

Formula: [Role/Identity] | [Who I help or what I do] | [Proof or differentiator]. Drop "aspiring" and "in progress" entirely. You're an ML engineer in training who ships, not a hopeful.

Option 1 · Keyword-optimized
ML Engineer in Training | Python · Deep Learning · Computer Vision | Building CV projects with TensorFlow & OpenCV | B.Tech AI '27
Use when: you want maximum search visibility. Front-loads the exact terms recruiters filter for. Best default for internship hunting.
Option 2 · Value-proposition
I build computer vision models that turn images into decisions | Python · TensorFlow · OpenCV | AI/ML undergrad seeking a 2026 ML internship
Use when: you want a human to feel what you do, not just scan keywords. Great if you're reaching out to people directly or attracting mentors.
Option 3 · Authority / thought-leadership
Learning ML in public | Documenting the road from B.Tech AI student to Machine Learning Engineer | Deep Learning · Computer Vision · Python
Use when: you plan to post regularly. "In public" invites people to follow the journey and works well with the 7-day posting plan below.

The "Value Comment" formula

Agree + Add Insight + Ask a Question. Example on a post about CNNs: "Totally agree data quality beats model size (Agree). I saw this first-hand, my accuracy jumped more from cleaning labels than from a bigger net (Insight). Curious, do you augment before or after your train/val split? (Question)". This gets you seen by the poster AND their audience, far better than "Great post!"
